Featured Capstone — Task 4

Sales Analytics & Business Intelligence Report

A full-year retail sales dataset — 1,000 transactions, 947 customers, 5 product categories — turned into segmentation insight, category performance, and a set of business recommendations.

Customer Segmentation
High-value customers, just one of three segments, drove 65.8% of total revenue — the clearest signal in the dataset for where retention efforts should focus.
Category Performance
Electronics led every category at ₹50.78M in revenue, with Electronics and Education together accounting for over 54% of total sales.
Seasonal Trend
Monthly revenue peaked in March 2025 at ₹13.06M, with Q4 showing sustained strength — useful signal for inventory and campaign timing.
Business Recommendation
Retain high-value customers through loyalty programs, expand Electronics, and apply targeted promotions to lift the underperforming Fashion category.
"Data-driven decision-making empowers businesses to understand customer behavior, monitor performance, and make informed strategic decisions that drive sustainable growth."
